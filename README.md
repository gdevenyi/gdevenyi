## 🌍 About Me

I build and maintain the computational backbone of a neuroimaging research lab —
turning messy multi-modal brain-imaging data into **reproducible, automated, and
scalable** analysis. My work sits at the intersection of **systems administration,
scientific software engineering, and high-performance computing**, so researchers
spend time on science instead of fighting their tools.

- 🔭 Building reproducible neuroimaging pipelines and the HPC/workstation infra behind them
- 🌱 Currently working on local LLM inference and GGUF sizing tooling
- 👯 Open to collaborating on open, FAIR, reproducible research software
- 💬 Ask me about neuroimaging pipelines, Ansible, containers, or Slurm

---

## 🧭 Research Focus

- ⚙️ **Reproducible pipelines** — versioned, containerized neuroimaging workflows
- 🖥️ **Research HPC** — Slurm clusters, standalone workstations, storage
- 🧰 **Automation** — Ansible-driven, one-command lab infrastructure
- 🧠 **Image processing** — registration, segmentation, atlasing (ANTs, MINC, ITK)
- 📦 **FAIR data** — BIDS-organized, portable, share-ready datasets
